# Boreas – privacy policy

Effective: 16 October 2026

[Polski](./)

## Who publishes the app

The Boreas app for Android and iOS is published by MobidevStudio Damian Ogórek. For privacy questions, write to: [mobidevstudio.dev@gmail.com](mailto:mobidevstudio.dev@gmail.com).

## In short

- The app collects, sends and shares no data.
- It doesn't connect to the internet. It has no analytics, ads, tracking or accounts.
- It asks for no permissions.

## What stays on your device

To work, the app saves in its own storage on your device:

- the language you chose;
- that the introduction has been shown;
- your reading progress through the sections;
- your saved sections;
- the items you ticked in the crisis plan;
- up to five recent searches.

The app never sends this data off your device. No one but you can access it, the app's publisher included.

## Backups

A system backup (Google backup on Android, iCloud or a computer backup on iOS) may include this data along with the rest of your device's data. The backup is kept in your Google or Apple account, under those companies' terms. You turn it on and off in your system settings.

## Calls and links

- Tapping an emergency number opens your phone's dialer with the number filled in. You decide whether to call. The app never places a call itself.
- A link in the app opens in your browser. The site you visit has its own privacy policy.

## Crash reports

If you agreed in your system settings to share diagnostics with app developers, Apple or Google may pass anonymous crash reports to the publisher. The system collects and sends them, not the app. The app adds no data to them.

## Deleting your data

- Uninstalling the app removes all its data from your device.
- In the app: “Remove” in Saved removes a saved section, “Clear” in Search removes your recent searches, and tapping a plan item again unticks it.

## Children

The app collects no data from anyone, children included.

## Independence

This app is independent. It is not published, endorsed or approved by MON, MSWiA, RCB or any other government body.

Its content comes from the public Polish “Poradnik bezpieczeństwa” (Safety guide).

## Changes

A new version of this policy will be published at the same address, with a new effective date.
